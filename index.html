📜 Rules of the Crypt

  • Format: The game has 2 stages (Stage 1: Python, Stage 2: Logic Puzzles).
  • Strict Timings: You have exactly 5 minutes per stage. If the timer hits zero, you will be forced to the next screen automatically.
  • One Attempt Only: There are no retries, no second chances, and no page refreshes allowed.
  • No Going Back: The next question only opens once you answer the current one. Once you select or submit an answer, it is permanently locked. You cannot go back.
  • Anti-Cheat System: Do not exit full screen, switch tabs, or copy-paste. You will get exactly 3 warnings. On the 4th warning, you will be instantly disqualified with a score of zero.
